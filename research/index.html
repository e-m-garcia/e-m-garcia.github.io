Mechanistic interpretability July 2026

J-Lens awareness is response-order dependent

I inject single-token Jacobian Lens vectors into Qwen 3.6–27B and test whether changing the order of “answer” and “report” changes the model’s stated detection of an intervention.

  • 3,120 positive-strength trials across both response orders
  • 322 → 0 exact reports when the report moves before the answer
  • 450 vs. 454 steered answers—nearly unchanged by order
